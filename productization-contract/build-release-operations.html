Build & Release Operations — RACI Matrix

Responsibility assignment for Productization and Component Teams based on the proposed contract
Source: Build & Release Operations Contract (Work In Progress) · Refreshed 25 Sep 2026
Guiding principles (from the document):
• Productization team will own implementation & maintenance of all automated tools, jobs, pipelines, documentation, and runbooks mentioned in each proposal.
• Productization owns the underlying infrastructure and global configuration; component / product teams own anything component-specific and related to product functionality.
R Responsible — does the work
A Accountable — ultimately answerable / approves
C Consulted — provides input before work
I Informed — kept updated on outcome
Compound assignments: When an area involves only two teams, a single team may carry two roles (e.g. RA = does the work and is ultimately accountable). All four RACI roles are covered in every row.
